What this does

Every row on an intake form has a tiny QR anchor plus 9 device-type checkboxes. Uploading a scan locates each row's anchor and reads which device-type box was ticked — then builds an editable preview where you type the serial & model.

On confirm

Checked rows become new assets with the asset tag you type in the preview (blank = auto INTK-<code>-<n>). Rows from an employee-bound form are set to Assigned to that employee; generic forms stay Available.

Assign per employee

Pick the employee whose printed form you scanned — confirmed assets become Assigned to them automatically with an accountability record.

Tip

Handwriting is never read automatically — the checkboxes only tell us the device type; you fill the asset tag and serial/model in the preview.
