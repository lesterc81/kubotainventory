help_outline How it works
What this does

Every row on a printed employee inventory form carries a signed QR token. Uploading a scan of the completed paper form decodes those tokens and records a per-row verification — it never silently changes an asset's status.

What gets updated

For each verified row we set the asset's last verified stamp, append an inventory-verification entry to its history, log an audit entry, and keep a copy of the scan for the record.

Tip

If the rows don't import, re-scan at higher resolution or hold the phone closer and steadier. Hand-written serial numbers are never read — only the printed QR tokens.
